How councils actually buy this work

Worth saying out loud, because most electrical contractors' websites are written as though a buyer is going to find them on Google and ring up.

Step one

Pre-approval, not advertising

Council work runs through procurement portals such as NEPO. You are either registered and pre-qualified or you are not asked. No amount of advertising shortcuts it.

Step two

The selection questionnaire

Insurance, accreditation, policies and comparable contracts, every time. CHAS Advanced and Principal Contractor status are what gets looked for.

Step three

The check they do anyway

Somebody will still look you up before shortlisting. ⭐ That is what this website is for — not to generate enquiries, but to survive the look.
